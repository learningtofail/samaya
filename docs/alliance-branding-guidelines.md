# Kingshot Alliance Branding Guidelines

Alliances can set a visual identity on the public event schedule. Your crest and color replace the generic defaults.

## 1. Alliance crest

Your crest appears on your filter chip, on your events, and, on your alliance's own page, large in the page header. The page crops it to a shield shape.

- **Format:** SVG (preferred), PNG, JPEG or GIF, at most 256 KB.
- **Shape:** a square (1:1) image. The shield crop trims the bottom corners, so keep important detail near the top and center.
- **Size:** it renders from 22x25 pixels (filter chips) up to 52x60 (page header). Make sure it stays readable at the small end. Avoid fine detail and small text.
- **Fallback:** without a crest, the page shows a shield with your alliance's initials on your color.

## 2. Brand color

Provide one hex color, six digits, for example `#E65100`.

- **Where it shows:** your selected filter chip, the highlight on the "next up" card, the active view toggle when your alliance is selected, and the accent stripe on your events.
- **Text color:** you do not provide one. The page uses pure white or pure black on your color, whichever reads better. Every valid color reaches at least 4.5:1 contrast this way, so no color is rejected for readability.
- **Contrast on the page background:** a very light color (pale yellow, for example) will show as a faint stripe against the white page. Pick a color that stands out on white.
- Status colors (Live now, Failed, Cancelled) are fixed by the platform and never take your color.

## 3. Submission

1. Post your crest file and hex color in the `#web-assets` Discord channel.
2. A server superadmin reviews them and uploads them in the Platform tenant editor.
3. The change shows for visitors on their next page load.

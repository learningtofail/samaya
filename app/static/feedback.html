About this page
Upvoting here is per-browser, not per-person: the first time you visit, this page stores a random anonymous id in your browser (nothing that identifies you). Clearing your browser's storage, or voting from a different browser or device, resets your vote weight — that's an accepted tradeoff for a page with no login, not a bug. An optional contact you leave on a submission is shown only to admins triaging the list, never on this public board.

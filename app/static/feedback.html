About this page

Upvotes are per browser, not per person: the first time you visit, this page stores a random anonymous id in your browser (nothing that identifies you). Clearing your browser's storage, or voting from a different browser or device, resets your vote. That is an accepted tradeoff for a page with no login, not a bug. An optional contact you leave on a submission is shown only to admins triaging the list, never on this board.
